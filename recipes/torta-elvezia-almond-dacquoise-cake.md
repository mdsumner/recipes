# Torta Elvezia (Almond Dacquoise Cake)

Source: https://www.bakinglikeachef.com/italian-almond-cake-elvezia-montersino/

Yield: 12-13
Prep 1 h 45 min | Cook 15 min | Total 2 h

## Ingredients

- 1 cup + 3 1/2 tablespoons (300 g) egg whites (see note #1)
- 2/3 cup + 2 1/2 tablespoons (185 g) caster sugar
- 2 2/3 cups + 4 tablespoons (280 g) almond flour
- 2/3 cup + 4 tablespoons (205 g) caster sugar
- 7 1/2 tablespoons (75 g) rice flour
- 2/3 cup + 3 1/2 tablespoons (200 g) granulated sugar
- 6 1/2 large (112 g) egg yolks (see note #2)
- 4 tablespoons (60 g) water
- 1/2 tablespoon (10 g) acacia honey
- 14.1 oz. (400 g) butter, softened
- 1/2 vanilla bean
- 7 oz. (200 g) toasted flaked almonds
- 1 tablespoon icing sugar

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 355 F (180 C). To make the almond dacquoise, place egg whites and 3/4 cup plus 1 tablespoon (185 g) caster sugar in a saucepan and beat with a hand whisk. Continue whisking and bring the mixture to low heat to warm.
2. Remove the egg whites/sugar mixture from the heat and beat with an electric or stand mixer on low speed. Gradually increase to medium speed and whisk for 5-7 minutes, then finish at high speed to achieve a glossy meringue.
3. Place almond flour, 3/4 cup + 2 1/2 tablespoons (205 g) caster sugar, and rice flour in a food processor bowl, and crush the dry mixture until homogeneous. Add this mixture to the meringue in 2 to 3 additions and mix with a rubber spatula.
4. Prepare three parchment paper sheets and draw three discs with a diameter of 8.5 inches (22 cm). Flip these paper sheets so the drawn circles face down.
5. Transfer the preparation to a pastry bag fitted with Ateco plain pastry tip 809 and pipe three dacquoise disks in a spiral shape within the pencil marks. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes. Remove the ready dacquoise disks from the oven and let them cool.
6. place the egg yolks and scraped vanilla seeds in a stand mixer bowl and whisk. In a saucepan, place water, honey (microwave for a few seconds in advance), and granulated sugar, and bring to a boil.
7. Once the syrup reaches 250 F (121 C), pour it into the egg yolks while whisking. Once the mixture cools and turns whitish, add the softened butter cubes one at a time, whisking until the cream is smooth and homogeneous.
8. trim the edges of the dacquoise disks with a knife to create perfect circles. Cover the first dacquoise disk with the buttercream, and sprinkle with toasted almonds.
9. Place the second disk and repeat. Finally, place the third disk on the cake and cover the top and edges with buttercream. Sprinkle the cake all over with flaked almonds. Dust the top of the cake with icing (powdered) sugar. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours.

## Notes

- 300 g egg whites, approximately equal to 9 raw fresh egg whites from large-sized chicken eggs.
- 112 g egg yolks, approximately equal to 6.5 raw fresh egg yolks from large-sized chicken eggs.
- Don't overmix the dacquoise mixture: it must remain airy before baking.
- Spread the meringue mixture with an offset spatula rather than making a spiral with a pastry bag and tip.
- Roast sliced almonds in the oven at 300 F (150 C) for 15 minutes.
- Serve the cake at room temperature: take it out of the fridge 30 minutes before serving.
- Make-ahead option: Bake dacquoise disks in the evening and keep them in a cool place. Assemble the cake the next day.
