# Nasturtium pesto

Source: https://larderlove.com/nasturtium-pesto/

Prep 10 min | Cook 1 min | Total 11 min

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup flaked almonds
- 1 cup nasturtium flowers
- 1 cup nasturtium leaves
- 1 clove garlic (minced)
- 1/4 cup parmesan (grated)
- salt and pepper
- 1/2 cup extra virgin olive oil

## Method

1. Toast the flaked almonds in dry pan for 1 minute till golden and aromatic then remove from pan immediately and allow to cool
2. Whazz all ingredients in food processor drizzling in the oil till you have the consistancy you prefer
