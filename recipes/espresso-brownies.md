# Espresso Brownies

Source: https://www.cookingforengineers.com/recipe/327/Espresso-Brownies

Yield: 1 8x8-in (20 cm) pan
Bake 30-40 min | cool completely in the pan

## Ingredients

- 4 oz (115 g) unsalted butter
- 1 cup (200 g) sugar
- 1/4 tsp (2.5 mL) vanilla extract
- 1 shot (4 Tbsp, 60 mL) fresh brewed espresso or very strong coffee
- 2 large (100 g) eggs
- 1/2 cup (80 g) all-purpose flour
- 1/3 cup (80 g) Hershey's cocoa powder
- 1/4 tsp (1.3 g) baking soda
- 1/4 tsp (1.5 g) table salt

## Method

1. Butter and flour an 8x8-inch pan; heat the oven to 350 F (175 C).
2. Melt the butter.
3. Stir in the sugar, vanilla and espresso.
4. Beat in the eggs.
5. Fold in the flour, cocoa, baking soda and salt until just combined.
6. Spread in the pan and bake 30-40 minutes. Leave to cool completely in the pan, which can take hours.

## Notes

- The source card gives 170 C for 350 F; 350 F is about 177 C, so 175 C here.
- Baking soda, not baking powder: an early version of the source said powder and was corrected.
- A glass pan gives a gooier result.
